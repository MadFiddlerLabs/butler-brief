# Privacy

Butler Brief collects nothing. It is one Claude Code skill: instructions for Claude, with no
server, no scripts and no analytics.

- **What it stores:** your household file, your to-dos and each day's brief, as plain text files
  in a `morning-brief/` folder on your computer. Delete the folder to remove them.
- **What it reads:** that folder; your calendar, only through a calendar connector you've added
  yourself and only after you say yes; and one web search for your town's weather, only after you
  say yes.
- **What it sends:** nothing to AELaboratories or Build Your Butler. Claude itself processes your
  messages under your own agreement with Anthropic.
